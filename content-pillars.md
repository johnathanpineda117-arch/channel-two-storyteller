# Channel 2 — Content Pillars

> **Legacy direction.** These five pillars belong to the original broad
> nonfiction storytelling definition of Channel 2, now registered as the
> retired `legacy-storyteller` channel. Current Channel 2 is RobloxTales /
> Block Tales, whose pillars are defined in [channels.md](channels.md).
>
> This document is preserved because these definitions remain the authoritative
> meaning of the legacy pillar terms, which historical records still use and
> which the knowledge catalog still cites. No active channel may use them.

These five pillars define the initial content universe for Channel 2.

They are not permanent assumptions.

Each pillar will be tested through actual Shorts performance and adjusted based on analytics.

---

## 1. 🧠 Human Stories

### Focus
Stories involving human behavior, relationships, dating, friendship, family, and social situations.

### Examples
- Unexpected relationship situations
- Friendship experiences
- Family moments
- Dating situations
- Human behavior
- Acts of kindness
- Betrayal or redemption
- Relatable social experiences

### Primary emotions
- ❤️ Emotion
- 😂 Humor
- 👀 Curiosity
- 🤯 Surprise

---

## 2. 😳 Unbelievable / Survival

### Focus
Stories involving survival, danger, animals, close calls, unusual real-world events, and situations that seem impossible.

### Examples
- How someone survived a bear attack
- Extreme survival stories
- Animal encounters
- Close calls
- Unbelievable rescues
- Unexpected survival situations

### Primary emotions
- 😳 Shock
- 🤯 Surprise
- 👀 Curiosity
- 😮 Relief

### Research requirement
Nonfiction claims must be researched and verified before being presented as factual.

---

## 3. 😂 Funny & Relatable

### Focus
Situations viewers recognize from their own lives or experiences with friends, family, work, dating, and everyday life.

### Examples
- Awkward situations
- Funny misunderstandings
- Friend experiences
- Dating moments
- Embarrassing situations
- Everyday social experiences
- Funny real-world stories

### Primary emotions
- 😂 Humor
- ❤️ Recognition
- 👀 Curiosity

### Community goal
Create situations that naturally make viewers think:

"That happened to me."

or

"Me and my friends have done this."

---

## 4. 🤯 Mystery / Strange

### Examples

# Pillar Strategy

The five pillars should provide variety while maintaining a recognizable Channel 2 identity.

The channel should feel like:

"Different stories, same emotional experience."

Pillars should not be treated equally by default.

Analytics will determine:

- Which pillars attract viewers
- Which pillars retain viewers
- Which pillars generate engagement
- Which pillars generate shares
- Which pillars create returning viewers
- Which pillars are easiest to produce consistently
- Which pillars are worth scaling

---

# Testing Philosophy

Initial content should be distributed across the five pillars to collect baseline data.

A pillar should not be declared successful or unsuccessful from one video.

Multiple videos and formats should be tested before making major decisions.

The goal is to identify:

1. Strong pillars
2. Strong story formats within each pillar
3. Strong hooks
4. Strong emotional payoffs
5. Strong visual styles
6. Repeatable winning combinations

Winning combinations should receive additional testing and production.

Underperforming combinations should be modified, retested, or eventually removed.

---

# Experimental Expansion

The five pillars are the initial foundation.

New content categories may be introduced later when:

- Analytics indicate an opportunity
- Audience behavior suggests demand
- A new format has strong potential
- A trend can be adapted without compromising channel identity

New categories should be treated as experiments before becoming permanent pillars.
